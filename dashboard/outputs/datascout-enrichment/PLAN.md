# DataScout enrichment acceptance plan

Baseline: c6621520. Scope: Matchs, Joueurs and Clubs, using docs/DATASCOUT_BENCHMARK.md including its premium-widget inventory. No team builder, optimal XI, editable formation or transfer-squad simulator. Keep production palette/Inter/branding, country selectors, aggregate player filtering, match details and Top 30 placeholders. Preserve parallel collection changes.

Failure modes and expected outcomes before implementation:

- Aggregate by stable player/match identifiers once. Totals, means and per-90 use known observations and their corresponding minutes. Ratios use summed numerators/denominators. Zero and missing remain distinct. Existing Raphinha reference (10 LaLiga matches, 723 minutes, 8.612 rating) remains reproducible.
- Centiles, role profiles, custom indices and similarity share documented position cohorts/minutes/coverage/tie rules. Do not rank omitted-zero xA as an unbiased distribution. Unknown or inadequate samples yield an explicit unavailable score, not a zero or silently reweighted score. Custom weights/directions/contributions are inspectable. Indices are descriptive and do not populate the official Top 30.
- Similarity excludes the player, uses enough jointly observed comparable features, and preserves stable IDs. Show the metric evidence and sample. Club-target analysis compares observed role peers; it is not a transfer-performance prediction.
- Players: one row per player across all views; configurable columns, free scatter axes, custom-index controls, four-player comparison, median and prior-season references, season evolution, shortlist(s), saved views and data/image/print exports. Search/stat filters apply after aggregation. Changing views, axes, columns or comparison selection must not narrow the cohort accidentally or drop selections.
- Persistence: local lists, index/comparison templates and views survive reload; reset/delete/import/export act on the named collection only. Validate schema/size/IDs on imported JSON. Storage failure remains usable. Never include provider keys or raw match payloads in saved preferences.
- Player history and archived fixtures are loaded only when requested, through small routing metadata and existing caches. No new full-season downloads on initial player directory render. Current filters, historical season and match-native season remain distinct.
- Clubs: season results/form, goals/xG for/against, venue splits, formation usage, squad minutes/starters/rotation, observed coaches, goal timing and style summaries use only applicable known data. Unreliable team stats are excluded from derived averages while scores remain usable. Club comparison and similar clubs show their sample/context, not an official strength ranking.
- Match context uses only fixtures/observations before kickoff. Never let later matches affect historical pre-match form, rest, comparison or head-to-head. Current/historical/upcoming/unknown-competition links work. Keep all existing tabs and raw detail families. Event summaries retain time, team and source; no invented event data.
- UI: organize content with named accessible tabs and short contextual actions. No new main-nav pages, helper-copy walls or empty premium teasers. Existing comparison remains available. Desktop/mobile and light/dark have no document overflow; tables scroll internally. Filter focus halos stay removed. Keyboard navigation, modal close/return focus and empty/retry states work.
- Source gaps: tracking, ages/foot/height, contracts, injuries, transfer prices, validated predictive projections and DataScout proprietary formulas remain source/model dependencies. No invented values or disabled cards to fill space. Cover every audit function in docs/DATASCOUT_IMPLEMENTATION.md with implemented/existing/excluded/data-blocked status.

Verification: real local data and independent raw calculations, production build, browser E2E only. Save exact setup/rerun commands, inputs/URLs, expected and actual values, original failures/corrections, console logs, downloads and screenshots in this folder. A build alone is not feature acceptance.
